Delete your Two Spoons account

How to delete your account and data from the Two Spoons app.

In the app

  1. Open Two Spoons and go to Today.
  2. Tap Settings (top right).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email twospoons.signin@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Two Spoons account". We'll delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and sign-in details.
  • Everything you wrote: answers, shared moments, thank-you notes, check-ins, game answers, your intimacy map and wish list.
  • Your link with your spouse, your invite codes and this phone's notification token.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Your spouse keeps their own account and what they wrote themselves.
