Help

I didn't get a sign-in code. Check your spam folder, then tap "Send a new code". Codes expire after an hour.

How do I link with my spouse? On Today, tap "+ Invite spouse", create a code and send it. Your spouse signs in on their own phone, taps "+ Invite spouse" and enters it under "I have a code". Codes work once and expire after 48 hours.

How do I unlink or delete my account? Open Settings (top right of Today). "Unlink" removes what you wrote from your shared space. "Delete my account" removes everything permanently.
