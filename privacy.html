Privacy policy

Two Spoons · Effective 12 November 2026

Two Spoons is an app for couples. It is built to keep what you write between you and your spouse. This policy explains what we collect, why, who handles it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to send you a one-time sign-in code. We never ask for a password.
  • The name you enter, shown to your spouse.
  • What you write and choose in the app: daily question answers, good moments, thank-you notes, weekly check-ins, intimacy exercises and wish-list choices, conversation notes, and game answers.
  • A push notification token for your phone, if you turn on notifications from your spouse.
  • Basic technical records our hosting provider keeps to run and secure the service, such as sign-in times and IP addresses.

Reminder settings stay on your phone. If you use Face ID or a fingerprint to lock the app, your phone checks it; it is never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • Notifications never include what you or your spouse wrote, only that something happened (for example, "Sam answered today's question").

What your spouse sees

Once you link with your spouse using an invite code, the two of you share a private space. Some things are hidden until you both take part: your spouse sees your daily answer, check-in, exercise answers and game answers only after giving their own. On the wish list, only the ideas you both chose are shown, never the ones only one of you picked. These rules are enforced on our server, not just in the app.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, on servers in Canada. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers sign-in code emails.
  • Expo, Apple and Google deliver push notifications. These contain no content you wrote.

We only share information with these providers so the app can work, and with authorities if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Unlink (Settings) deletes everything you wrote from the space you shared with your spouse.
  • Delete my account (Settings) permanently deletes your account and everything you wrote. It cannot be undone.
  • Otherwise, we keep your information while your account exists.

You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Age

Two Spoons is for adults in a relationship and is not meant for anyone under 18. It includes optional content about sex and intimacy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app.
